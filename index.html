The process

From evidence pack to auditable output.

Four stages. Each with a defined input, a defined output, and a human review point. The Scheme Actuary retains sign-off at the critical gate — nothing enters the compute path without approval.

01

Ingest

Trust Deed and Rules as amended, interim deeds, factor tables, worked examples, reconciliation spreadsheets. No pretence that the documents are clean.

02

Structure

Frontier AI models produce structured candidates — typed rule objects, factor references, methodology choices, provenance to source. Weeks of interpretation cut to hours.

03

Validate

The Scheme Actuary reviews the structured benefit specification and approves it. Contradictions between source documents surface as open items, not silent assumptions.

04

Compute and audit

Deterministic runtime. No AI in the calculation path. Outputs replay against worked examples with documented tolerances on every rebuild.

Built to be defensible

Five answers to the question every serious buyer asks.

01

The Scheme Actuary owns the deliverable

The actuary reviews the benefit specification, makes the judgement calls, and signs off the output. Professional accountability does not move.

02

No AI in the calculation runtime

The runtime is deterministic. The same inputs produce the same outputs every time. AI accelerates structuring — it plays no part in the calculation itself.

03

Every output testable against worked examples

A regression harness asserts outputs match worked examples on every rebuild. Test failure surfaces before anything reaches the member population.

04

Provenance back to the deed

Every methodology choice and calculation parameter traces back to the specific deed clause or statutory provision that governs it.

05

Member data never touches the AI

Anonymisation is built into the pipeline before any AI access. The structuring step sees only scheme structure and rule text, not member records.
