The process

Four stages from evidence pack to auditable output.

Every Diorama engagement follows the same four-stage discipline. Each stage has a defined input, a defined output, and a human review point before anything moves forward. The Scheme Actuary retains sign-off at the critical gate.

01

Ingest

Diorama starts from the evidence pack as it actually exists. Trust Deed and Rules as amended. Interim deeds. Factor tables. Worked examples from prior implementations. Reconciliation spreadsheets. Admin notes that explain the gaps. Prior calculation outputs where they can be found.

There is no clean starting point requirement. The complexity of the source material is the input, not an obstacle. The first stage gathers everything that describes how the scheme is meant to work, without filtering or sanitising what comes in.

No pretence that the documents are clean. Diorama starts from real evidence packs: the deeds as amended, the spreadsheets as they are, the reconciliation notes from a decade ago.
02

Structure

Frontier AI models read the evidence pack and produce structured candidates: typed rule objects, benefit category definitions, factor table extracts, methodology choices, provenance back to the specific deed clause or statutory provision at each step. The candidates are not the final benefit specification. They are the raw material for human review.

The acceleration is at the front of the process: turning weeks of manual deed interpretation into hours of structured output that the Scheme Actuary and the implementation team can interrogate, challenge, and approve. Nothing moves to implementation without that sign-off.

The time from messy evidence pack to structured benefit specification ready for Scheme Actuary review drops from weeks to hours.
03

Validate

Candidates go through the engine's discipline layer. Required fields must be present. Date logic must be coherent. Factor table coverage must be complete. Methodology choices must be explicit. Contradictions between source documents surface as open items, not silent assumptions. Every gap in the evidence pack is visible before implementation starts.

The Scheme Actuary reviews the structured basis and approves it before anything enters the compute path. This is the point where professional judgement operates. The engine surfaces the choices. The actuary makes them and signs off.

The Scheme Actuary reviews and approves the benefit specification. Nothing enters the compute path without that sign-off.
04

Compute and audit

The approved benefit specification enters a deterministic runtime. The same inputs produce the same outputs every time. No AI in the calculation path. Outputs replay against worked examples with documented tolerances: a regression harness that runs automatically on every rebuild. Test failure surfaces before anything reaches the member population.

Calculation artefacts are delivered in the consumer's own code conventions: Python modules, Excel proformas, regression harnesses, calculation packs, audit trails. No platform migration required. The output is not just a calculation — it is a calculation with its own verification evidence.

The same inputs produce the same outputs, every time. Deterministic runtime. No AI in the calculation path.
