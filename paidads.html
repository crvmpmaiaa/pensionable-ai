Why paid advertising works and when it's the right move

SEO builds long-term authority. Paid advertising builds immediate visibility. Both have their place but if you need leads now, paid ads are the fastest way to put your business in front of people who are actively looking for what you offer.

The appeal of paid advertising is simple: you show up at the exact moment someone is searching for your product or service, or you reach the exact type of person most likely to buy from you on social media. When it's managed well, every pound spent returns a multiple in revenue.

200% average ROI on Google Ads £2 returned for every £1 spent
65% of people click on ads when they're ready to buy
4x more likely to click a paid ad than organic for high-intent searches

The businesses that get the most from paid ads are the ones who treat it as a system not a set-and-forget expense. That means constant testing, optimisation, and a clear view of what's working and what isn't.

Google Ads capturing people who are ready to buy

Google Search Ads are the most powerful form of intent-based advertising available. When someone searches "web designer near me" or "best SEO agency," they're not browsing they're actively looking to spend money. Google Ads puts you at the top of those results, instantly.

The difference between a profitable Google Ads campaign and a money pit comes down to how well it's structured and managed. We handle every element:

  • Keyword strategy: targeting the searches that signal buying intent, not just traffic
  • Match types: controlling exactly when your ads show to avoid wasted spend
  • Negative keywords: blocking irrelevant searches that would burn your budget
  • Ad copy: compelling headlines and descriptions that earn the click
  • Landing page alignment: the page someone lands on must match the promise of the ad
  • Bid strategy: automated and manual bidding adjusted to maximise your return

The average business wastes up to 25% of its Google Ads budget on irrelevant clicks. Proper campaign structure and ongoing management eliminates that waste from day one.

Meta Ads reaching the right people on Facebook and Instagram

While Google captures demand, Meta creates it. Facebook and Instagram ads put your brand in front of people who match your ideal customer profile even before they know they need you. When done well, it's one of the most cost-effective ways to build brand awareness and drive sales simultaneously.

Meta's advertising platform gives us access to some of the most granular audience targeting available anywhere online. We use it to find and convert your ideal customer:

  • Interest and behaviour targeting: reaching people whose online activity signals they're likely buyers
  • Lookalike audiences: finding new customers who mirror your existing best ones
  • Custom audiences: targeting your existing customers, email list, or website visitors
  • Instagram integration: seamless campaigns across both platforms from one ad account
  • Video and carousel formats: high-performing ad formats that stop the scroll

Meta ads have become increasingly powerful for e-commerce, local businesses, and service providers alike. The key is knowing which audience, format, and offer works for your specific business and testing relentlessly until you find it.

Ad creative why most campaigns fail before they start

You can have the most precise targeting in the world and still get no results if your creative is weak. The ad itself the image or video, the headline, the copy is what stops someone mid-scroll and makes them care. It's the thing that earns the click.

Most agencies focus on the technical setup and phone it in on creative. We don't. Every campaign we run includes:

  • Ad copywriting: headlines and body copy written to convert, not just inform
  • Creative strategy: deciding what angle, hook, and format will resonate with your audience
  • Multiple variants: we test different creatives to find what performs best
  • AI-generated imagery: high-quality visuals produced fast and cost-effectively using AI tools
  • Video creative: short-form video consistently outperforms static on Meta platforms

Creative accounts for up to 70% of a campaign's performance. Better creative means better results without spending an extra penny on media.

Targeting and audiences reaching the right people

Broad targeting wastes budget. Overly narrow targeting limits reach. The sweet spot is precise but scalable reaching people who are genuinely likely to become customers, in enough volume to make the campaign viable.

We build audience strategies specific to your business and goals:

  • Demographic targeting: age, location, income, job title where relevant
  • Geographic targeting: local, national, or international depending on your reach
  • In-market audiences Google's data on people actively researching purchases in your category
  • Customer match: uploading your customer list to find similar buyers
  • Seasonal adjustments: shifting targeting and budget around your peak periods

Retargeting converting the people who almost bought

Most people who visit your website for the first time don't buy. That's normal they're researching, comparing, or just not quite ready yet. Retargeting lets you stay in front of those people with relevant ads until they are ready.

It's the most cost-efficient advertising you can run because you're only spending money on people who've already shown interest in you. Conversion rates are significantly higher, cost per acquisition is lower, and it reinforces your brand at exactly the right moment.

  • Website visitor retargeting following up with people who visited key pages
  • Cart abandonment campaigns for e-commerce
  • Video view retargeting following up with people who watched your ads
  • Cross-platform retargeting someone sees you on Google, then on Instagram
  • Sequential messaging telling a story across multiple touchpoints
10x higher click-through rate for retargeted ads vs standard display
70% of visitors who are retargeted are more likely to convert
3-5x lower cost per acquisition compared to cold audience campaigns

Tracking and reporting knowing what's actually working

One of the most common problems we see with existing campaigns is that nobody actually knows whether they're working. Impressions and clicks are easy metrics to report on revenue and cost per acquisition are the ones that matter.

Before we spend a single penny of your budget, we make sure tracking is properly set up:

  • Google Analytics 4: full visibility of how ad traffic behaves on your site
  • Conversion tracking: measuring the actions that actually matter to your business
  • Meta Pixel and CAPI: accurate attribution even with iOS privacy changes
  • Call tracking: for businesses where phone enquiries are a key conversion
  • Monthly reporting: clear, plain-English reports showing spend, leads, cost per lead, and ROAS

We don't hide behind vanity metrics. If something isn't working, we'll tell you and we'll fix it.
