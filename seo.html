Why SEO still matters (more than ever)

Every year someone declares SEO dead. Every year the businesses quietly investing in it pull further ahead. The truth is simple: when someone searches for what you offer, you either show up or you don't. And the businesses that show up get the leads, the calls, and the sales without paying per click.

Paid ads stop the moment you stop paying. SEO compounds. A page that ranks today can bring in leads for years. That's why it remains the highest long-term ROI marketing channel available to any business.

How SEO actually works

Google's job is to match searchers with the most relevant, trustworthy result. Your job with our help is to become that result for the searches that matter to your business.

There are three things Google weighs when deciding who ranks:

  • Relevance: does your page actually answer what the person searched for?
  • Authority: do other reputable sites link to and reference you?
  • Experience: is your site fast, secure, and easy to use on any device?

A proper SEO campaign works on all three simultaneously. Fixing just one rarely moves the needle. That's why shortcuts never stick and why agencies selling "guaranteed rankings in 30 days" are selling snake oil.

Technical SEO the foundation everything builds on

Before content or links can work, your site needs to be technically sound. Technical SEO is what Google sees under the hood and if it's broken, nothing else matters.

We audit and fix:

  • Site speed: slow sites lose rankings and visitors. We optimise images, code, and server performance
  • Mobile experience Google indexes mobile-first. If your site is poor on phone, your rankings suffer
  • Core Web Vitals Google's official page experience signals (LCP, FID, CLS)
  • Crawlability: making sure Google can find and index all the right pages
  • Schema markup: structured data that helps Google understand your content and display rich results
  • HTTPS and security: a basic trust signal that affects both rankings and conversions

Content strategy ranking for what your customers search

Content is how you tell Google what you're about. But it's not about writing articles for the sake of it. Every piece of content we produce starts with keyword research understanding exactly what your potential customers type into Google, and creating the best possible answer.

Our content strategy includes:

  • In-depth keyword research mapped to your services and customer journey
  • Competitor content gap analysis finding what they rank for that you don't
  • Service pages optimised to convert as well as rank
  • Blog and insight content that builds authority in your niche
  • Regular content updates to keep pages fresh and ranking

Good SEO content isn't stuffed with keywords. It genuinely answers questions, demonstrates expertise, and gives readers a reason to trust and contact you.

Link building the hardest part, done right

Links from other websites are still one of the strongest ranking signals Google uses. Think of each link as a vote of confidence the more quality votes you have, the more Google trusts you.

The keyword there is quality. Low-quality links from spammy sites can actively hurt your rankings. We focus on earning links that Google values:

  • Editorial links from relevant industry publications
  • Digital PR getting your business featured in news and blogs
  • Guest posting on authoritative sites in your sector
  • Local citations for geographically targeted businesses
  • Broken link building replacing dead links with your content

Link building takes time. Anyone promising hundreds of links overnight is building a liability, not an asset. We play the long game and the long game wins.

Local SEO dominating searches in your area

If your business serves a specific location a city, a region, or even a neighbourhood local SEO is one of the highest-value things you can invest in. Showing up in the Google Map Pack (the three businesses that appear above organic results) can transform your inbound enquiries.

Our local SEO work includes:

  • Google Business Profile optimisation and management
  • Local keyword targeting (e.g. "web designer in Manchester")
  • Review strategy more reviews, better reviews, consistently
  • Local citations ensuring your business details are consistent across the web
  • Location pages for businesses serving multiple areas
