Why cold outbound is still one of the best ways to grow

Most marketing channels require patience. SEO takes months. Ads need testing and budget. Content takes time to compound. Cold outbound is different. It's the fastest way to put your offer in front of a specific, qualified person and start a real conversation today.

When it's done properly, cold email and LinkedIn outreach are not spam. They are a direct, personal conversation with someone who fits your ideal customer profile. The difference between cold outbound that gets replies and cold outbound that gets ignored is almost entirely execution.

89% of marketers say email is their primary channel for generating leads

The businesses we build outbound systems for stop waiting for the phone to ring. They know exactly how many emails are going out, what the open rate is, and how many meetings that's generating. Outbound makes pipeline predictable.

Cold email at scale with Instantly

The reason most cold email campaigns fail is infrastructure. Sending bulk email from a single inbox gets you flagged and filtered before your prospects ever see your message. We build campaigns on Instantly, which solves this by distributing sends across multiple warmed inboxes and managing deliverability automatically.

What this means in practice: your emails actually arrive, they land in the primary inbox rather than spam, and you can send at a volume that produces real results without burning your domain.

  • Multi-inbox sending: distributing volume across multiple sending accounts so no single inbox gets flagged
  • Inbox warm-up: gradually building sender reputation before campaigns go live so deliverability is high from day one
  • Automated sequences: multi-step follow-up campaigns that keep reaching out until someone replies or opts out
  • A/B testing: testing subject lines, opening lines, and offers to find what gets the best response rate
  • Reply detection: sequences pause automatically when a prospect replies so no one gets a follow-up after they've already responded
  • Unified inbox: all replies across all sending accounts in one place so nothing gets missed

A properly configured Instantly campaign with warmed inboxes typically achieves 35-50% open rates. The industry average for cold email sitting on a single poorly configured account is under 20%.

LinkedIn outreach reaching decision-makers where they work

LinkedIn is where B2B buyers spend their professional time. A well-run LinkedIn outreach campaign puts your business directly in front of the decision-makers at the companies you want to work with. Combined with cold email, it creates multiple touchpoints that significantly increase the chance of a conversation happening.

We build LinkedIn outreach systems that feel personal at scale:

  • Connection campaigns: targeted connection requests to your ideal customer profile with personalised notes that get accepted
  • Message sequences: follow-up messages after connection that start a genuine conversation rather than pitching immediately
  • Profile optimisation: your LinkedIn profile needs to convert when someone checks who you are after your message lands
  • Sales Navigator targeting: using LinkedIn's advanced filters to build highly specific prospect lists by role, industry, company size, and location
  • Personalisation at scale: using dynamic fields and research triggers to make outreach feel individual even when sending volume is high

LinkedIn and cold email work best together. Someone who gets an email and then sees a connection request from the same person is far more likely to engage than someone who only gets one or the other.

Lead lists and ICP targeting who you contact matters as much as what you say

The most common reason cold outbound fails is bad targeting. Sending a well-written email to the wrong person is still a waste of time. We build lead lists around your ideal customer profile: the specific company size, industry, location, job title, and buying signals that indicate someone is actually likely to want what you offer.

  • ICP definition: getting precise about who your best customers are and working backwards to find more of them
  • Data sourcing: building lists from Apollo, Clay, Sales Navigator, and other sources with verified contact data
  • Email verification: cleaning lists before sending to remove invalid addresses that damage deliverability
  • Intent signals: targeting companies that are actively hiring, recently funded, or showing other signs of being ready to buy
  • Segmentation: splitting lists by persona or vertical so messaging can be tailored rather than generic

A targeted list of 500 well-researched prospects will outperform a generic list of 5,000 every time. Volume without precision is just noise.

Sequence copywriting the words that get replies

Cold email copy is one of the most misunderstood areas of marketing. The instinct is to write about your business, your service, and why you're great. What actually gets replies is the opposite: a short, specific, human message focused entirely on the prospect and what you can do for them.

Every sequence we write follows principles that consistently produce above-average reply rates:

  • Short subject lines: three to five words that read like a real email, not a marketing blast
  • One-sentence opener: a specific, relevant observation about the prospect or their business that shows you've done your homework
  • Clear value proposition: one sentence on what you do and who you do it for, with a result they care about
  • Soft call to action: asking for a conversation rather than a commitment. "Would this be worth a 15-minute call?" converts far better than "Book a demo"
  • Strategic follow-ups: each follow-up adds value rather than just bumping the thread. Different angles, different reasons to reply

We write and test everything. Subject lines, openers, CTAs, follow-up angles. The sequence that goes live is the one the data says will work best for your specific audience.

Deliverability the technical side most people get wrong

You can have a perfect list and brilliant copy and still get zero results if your emails are going to spam. Deliverability is the unsexy infrastructure work that determines whether any of this works. It's also where most DIY cold email campaigns fall apart.

  • Domain setup: using secondary sending domains rather than your primary business domain, so your main email reputation stays protected
  • DNS configuration: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records correctly configured for every sending domain
  • Inbox warm-up: new sending accounts go through a warm-up period using Instantly's automated warm-up network before live campaigns begin
  • Sending limits: staying within safe daily send volumes per inbox so no account trips spam filters
  • List hygiene: removing bounces and unsubscribes immediately to maintain a clean sender reputation
